FlowSync Pro - Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 18, 2026

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your FlowSync Pro account and associated data by following these steps:

Email us at: RaghavKathuria@devflow.me

Include the following in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Account credentials (email, password hash, display name, phone number)
  • All shipment data you created or participated in
  • Participant records and invite tokens
  • Contact records and trust scores
  • Escalation records
  • Audit logs associated with your actions
  • Analytics data linked to your account

Data Retained

The following data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Aganonymized analytics data (not personally identifiable)
  • Shipment records where you were a participant (other participants' data remains)
  • Legal/compliance records as required by law

Processing Time

We will process your account deletion request within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion, contact us at RaghavKathuria@devflow.me.
